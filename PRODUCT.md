# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
People and organisations looking to hire a photographer and cinematographer:

- Couples and families booking weddings, events and celebrations
- Brands and businesses
- Film and music clients
- Portrait and fashion clients
- Professionals who want personal coverage, such as travel and speaking at events

Their job on the site is to see work relevant to their need, decide they want Des Touch, and get in touch.

## Product Purpose
A portfolio for Des Touch, the brand of Teddy Sukpa. It shows his photography and cinematography and turns visitors into WhatsApp conversations. Success means qualified enquiries arriving on WhatsApp.

## Positioning
Undecided. To be drawn from Teddy's bio and real work, not invented.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Primary action: message Des Touch on WhatsApp.
- Photos and videos currently live on Google Drive. Drive stays the master archive and is not the delivery host for the site.
- The site must stay fast with heavy image and video content.
- Based in Bayelsa, Nigeria, and travels for shoots.
- No admin page or CMS. The site stays simple, and new work is added by editing the project directly.
- The site does not show prices. Pricing conversations happen on WhatsApp.
- Built in plain PHP with no database, chosen by the user, for ordinary shared hosting.
- Undecided: domain.

## Brand Commitments
- Public brand name: Des Touch.
- Person behind the brand: Teddy Sukpa.
- Logo: heavy black DES with TOUCH running through it, and a lime lens dot in the O. Originals are in `images/`, and the site uses the traced vector at `assets/img/logo.svg`.

## Evidence on Hand
The logo is in the project. Photos, videos, bio and contact details are reported to exist but have not been supplied. The site currently uses labelled Wikimedia Commons samples under made-up project names, credited on `/credits`, and these must be replaced before launch. Do not fabricate clients, testimonials, awards, prices or credits.

## Product Principles
1. The work leads; the interface stays out of its way.
2. Every type of client can quickly find work that looks like what they need.
3. WhatsApp is never more than a tap away, especially on phones.
4. Speed is part of the craft, since slow media undermines a visual portfolio.
